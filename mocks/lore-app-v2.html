Hypothesis
Attention mechanisms can replace recurrence entirely for sequence modelling tasks.
Methodology

Literature review of "Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017) and follow-up papers. Implemented a mini transformer on a toy dataset to validate the core claims.

Key Findings
1Self-attention scales quadratically with sequence length — a key bottleneck.
2Positional encodings are critical; learned vs sinusoidal perform similarly.
3Multi-head attention allows attending to different representation subspaces.
References
Attention Is All You Need — Vaswani et al. The Illustrated Transformer — Jay Alammar

C@claude What are the main architectural differences between encoder-only and decoder-only transformers?

C
Claude · Sonnet 4just now
Encoder-only models (BERT, RoBERTa) use bidirectional attention — ideal for classification and embeddings. Decoder-only models (GPT family) use causal/autoregressive attention, optimized for generation. Encoder-decoder (T5, BART) combines both via cross-attention between encoder states and the decoder.
🎯
Conclusion
Transformers are now the dominant architecture for NLP and increasingly vision tasks. Worth investing deep time in internals before moving to fine-tuning.
