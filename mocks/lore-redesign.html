Transformer Architecture Deep Dive

Machine Learning Research Notes
💡
Hypothesis

Attention mechanisms can replace recurrence entirely for sequence modelling tasks.

Methodology

Literature review of "Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017) and follow-up papers. Implemented a mini transformer on a toy dataset.

Key Findings
1 Self-attention scales quadratically with sequence length — a key bottleneck.
2 Positional encodings are critical; learned vs sinusoidal encodings perform similarly.
3 Multi-head attention allows the model to attend to different representation subspaces.
References
Attention Is All You Need — Vaswani et al. The Illustrated Transformer — Jay Alammar
🎯
Conclusion

Transformers are now the dominant architecture for NLP and increasingly for vision tasks. Worth investing time in understanding the internals.
