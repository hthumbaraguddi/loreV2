What are the main scaling challenges with transformer attention?
You · 14:22
The dominant challenge is the O(n²) complexity of scaled dot-product attention — both memory and compute grow quadratically with sequence length. This makes standard transformers impractical for very long documents (>8k tokens). Approaches like sparse attention (Longformer, BigBird), linear attention approximations, and FlashAttention (IO-aware) help significantly. Flash-Attention-3 is the current state of the art for hardware-efficient attention.
