AI Concept Explained
What Is an "Agentic AI"?

From Chatbots to Autonomous Agents

You've used AI to answer questions. But Agentic AI goes further — it can plan, use tools, browse the web, write code, and complete multi-step goals with minimal human input.

Think of it like the difference between asking a colleague a question vs. delegating an entire project to them.

  • 1Perception — The agent receives a goal: "Research competitors and draft a summary report."
  • 2Planning — It breaks the task into steps: search the web, read pages, identify patterns, write report.
  • 3Tool Use — It calls real tools: browser, calculator, database queries, email, code execution.
  • 4Self-correction — If a step fails, it retries with a different approach, just like a human would.
  • 5Output — The finished report lands in your inbox. You reviewed the goal; the agent did the work.
Pro Tip of the Week

When prompting AI for complex tasks, try the "Chain of Thought" technique — add "Think step by step before answering" to your prompt. Studies show this alone can improve AI accuracy by up to 40% on multi-step problems.
